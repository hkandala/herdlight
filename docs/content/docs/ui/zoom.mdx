---
title: "Zoom"
description: "Zooming one card to fill the window"
---

- The zoom button, a double-click on the header, or ⌘⇧↩ zooms a card. It is
  drawn in an opaque layer over the whole window under the tab bar. Only the
  zoomed card resizes.
- The same button or ⌘⇧↩ restores the layout. Esc is not used, because the
  program in the pane needs it.
