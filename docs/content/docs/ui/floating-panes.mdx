title: "Floating panes"
description: "Cards that float above the strip"
---

<MacWindow floating caption={<><b>Try:</b> drag the scratch card by its header, resize it from its corner, and press <b>⧉ 1</b> to hide or show it.</>} />

- Any pane (terminal, agent, web page, plugin) can float above the strip of
  its workspace. A floating card has the normal header plus **Dock** (⤓).
  Dock moves the pane into the selected tab as a right split, with the same
  call as a drop on that tab's capsule.
- Drag the header to move the card, or onto a tab capsule or card edge to
  dock it there. Drag the corner to resize. A click brings a card to the
  front.
- Layers, bottom to top: strip, floating cards, zoom layer. While a card is
  zoomed, the floating cards are hidden.
- ⌥⌘F or the `⧉ n` button shows or hides the workspace's floating panes.
  With none, it opens the new-pane picker. Return makes a floating scratch
  terminal.
- Hiding never closes: the process keeps running in herdr.
- iPhone: floating panes show as one more tab, "Floating".

How it works: [Floating panes in herdr](/docs/floating-panes).
