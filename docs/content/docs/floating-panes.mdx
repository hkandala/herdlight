## Where they live

A floating pane is a normal herdr pane in a tab labeled exactly `hl:float`.
Every pane in that tab floats. The app creates at most one such tab per
workspace. If two exist, the panes in both float, and new floating panes go
to the first.

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **a tab labeled `hl:float`** | **chosen**: one marker per workspace; any pane kind can float; herdr keeps the tab, its panes and labels across restarts; `pane.move` keeps the terminal; every device and the herdr TUI agree |
| herdr's popup | rejected: one per server, no pane id, not in the snapshot, not saved, modal on herdr TUI screens |
| a pane label marker | rejected: the label already holds `web:`/`hl:` or the user's own label |
| a metadata token | rejected: lost on restart |
| an app-side list | rejected: ids change on restart, and other devices would not agree |

- herdr TUI users see one more tab, "hl:float", with the floating panes as
  splits. That is where they live, and they can use them there.
- If a TUI user renames the tab, its panes show as docked.

## herdr calls

| User action | herdr call (all with `focus:false`) |
|---|---|
| New floating pane, no floating tab yet | `tab.create {workspace_id, cwd, label:"hl:float"}`, then the pane kind's create steps |
| New floating pane, floating tab exists | `pane.split` on the largest pane in that tab, alternating `right` and `down`, then the kind's steps; if herdr refuses, the app shows herdr's reason |
| Float a docked pane | `pane.move {pane_id, destination:{type:"tab", tab_id:<floating tab>, split:"right"}}`, or with no floating tab `pane.move {pane_id, destination:{type:"new_tab", workspace_id, label:"hl:float"}}` |
| Dock a floating pane | the normal moves of [Writes are herdr calls](/docs/talking-to-herdr#writes-are-herdr-calls); the **Dock** button does the same as a drop on the selected tab's capsule: `pane.move {pane_id, destination:{type:"tab", tab_id, split:"right"}}` |
| Close | `pane.close`; asks first unless the pane runs only its shell |

Tested on 0.9.3:
- `new_tab` with a label creates the tab and moves the pane in one call,
  without moving herdr's focus.
- Moving the last pane out closes the tab.
- The tab and its label come back after a cold restart.

## How the app shows them

- On macOS and iPad: cards drawn inside the main window, above the strip
  ([Floating panes](/docs/ui/floating-panes)). Not a separate window, because
  two windows that stream terminals would fight over control of the same
  panes.
- The floating tab is not a tab page: it is left out of the tab bar, the
  strip, ⌘1…9 and the lit-tab math.
- The app ignores that tab's split tree; each pane is its own card.
- Card position, size, shown or hidden, and stacking order are the app's own,
  in memory. After an app restart the floating panes are hidden, with their
  count on `⧉ n`, and open at default positions (centered, 70% × 75% of the
  strip, each next card 24 pt down and right).
- When a drag or resize ends, the card sends `terminal.resize`.
- A scroll that starts on a floating card goes to that card, never the strip.
- Floating agents appear under their workspace in the sidebar with a ⧉ mark.
  Clicking the row, a status row, a notification or a pill card's **Open in
  app** shows the
  floating panes and focuses that card.
- On iPhone the floating tab shows as one more tab, "Floating".

## Rules

- The one control rule
  ([One controller per pane](/docs/talking-to-herdr#one-controller-per-pane))
  applies. A shown floating card streams like the selected tab's cards. A
  hidden one releases its stream.
- Zooming a floating card draws it in the zoom layer, and the other floating
  cards are hidden. Unzooming returns it to its floating frame.
- Moving a pane between the strip and the floating layer keeps its
  registry entry, so its view moves without a reload.
- herdr's own popups are not shown: no public call reveals one. If herdr adds
  a popup field to the snapshot, the app can stream it by terminal id in
  about 40 lines.
